Palchha

A Village of Farmers

Palchha is a historical village located in Chittorgarh district, Rajasthan. The name Palchha is believed to have originated from the “Pal” (embankment) of a nearby pond, and even today, some remains of the old embankment can still be seen. In ancient times, Palchha was a small unit of the Chittorgarh princely state (Rehasat). The historical lineage of the region is traced from Chitragand Maurya → Chitrakoot → Chittorgarh, and Palchha was included within the circumference of the Chittorgarh Rehasat. During that era, the Bijaypur Zamindar, who came under the Chittorgarh Rehasat, collected different kinds of taxes from the villages. Even today, the people of the 52 villages that were once part of the Bijaypur Rehasat consider Narendra Singh Rao as their local king and continue to respect the royal family. Palchha has a current population of around 800 people, and the majority of the residents depend on agriculture for their livelihood. The village spans approximately 1000 Bigha (around 600 Acres) of fertile land and is known for its hardworking farming community, natural beauty, and peaceful lifestyle. There are also several ancient stone inscriptions (Shilalekh) in the village, especially near the bus stand and the temple, which reflect the historical identity of Palchha.

Main Temples in village

  1. Mata Ji Temple

    A highly respected sacred place where villagers pray for protection, peace, and prosperity. Navratri celebrations here are full of devotion and cultural energy.

  2. Hanuman Temple

    A temple symbolizing strength, courage, and devotion. Every Tuesday and Saturday, devotees gather here to recite the Hanuman Chalisa and offer prayers.

  3. Mahadev (Shiva) Temple

    A peaceful spiritual destination dedicated to Lord Shiva. Mondays and the full month of Shravan mark special rituals, bhajans, and large gatherings.

  4. Charbuja Nath Temple

    A significant temple dedicated to Lord Charbuja Nath (a form of Shri Vishnu). The temple is believed to fulfill devotees' wishes and is known for its calm and divine atmosphere. On special occasions and festivals villagers visit to seek blessings for family well‑being and prosperity.

Festivals

Palchha celebrates several local and regional festivals with devotion, music and community gatherings:

  • Navratri (Mata Ji)

    Nine nights of worship and cultural programs at the Mata Ji Temple — traditional garba, bhajans and large evening gatherings. This is one of the busiest festival times in the village.

  • Shravan (Mahadev)

    During the month of Shravan devotees visit the Mahadev (Shiva) Temple for special rituals, offerings and communal bhajans, especially on Mondays.

  • Hanuman Jayanti

    Special prayers and recitation of the Hanuman Chalisa at the Hanuman Temple, with community prasads and processions on some years.

  • Diwali & Makar Sankranti

    Celebrated across households with lamps, sweets and visits between families. Makar Sankranti is marked by community feasts and local kite-flying in open fields.

  • Bhajan Sandhya (Every Purnima)

    On every Purnima night a Bhajan Sandhya is organized at the Charbhuja Nath Mandir — devotees gather to sing devotional songs and offer prayers for peace and prosperity.

  • Jaljulni Gyaras

    Villagers celebrate with traditional rituals, devotional songs and processions. The festival reflects strong faith and unity among the people.

  • Teja Dashami

    Observed with devotion to Veer Teja Ji; a special procession is held and offerings are made for a good harvest and protection of cattle.

  • Bhagwat Katha

    Once a year priests narrate spiritual stories from the Shreemad Bhagwat; villagers attend the discourse and receive prasad at the end of the event.

  • Ganesh Chaturthi

    Villagers install idols of Lord Ganesha, perform prayers and participate in cultural programs. Community gatherings and visarjan are organized as part of the celebrations.

  • Janmashtami (Janasatmi)

    Celebration of Lord Krishna's birth with evening bhajans, fasting, and devotional programs at local temples.

  • Mahashivratri

    Major night-long worship of Lord Shiva at the Mahadev Temple with special abhishek, bhajans and large gatherings; villagers observe fasts and offer prayers for spiritual benefits.

  • Holi

    Colourful spring festival celebrated with colors, music and sweets. Neighbors and families gather to play Holi, share festive foods and strengthen community bonds.

Plan Your Visit

Best Time to Visit

October to March is the ideal season with pleasant weather. Avoid monsoon (July–September) and peak summer (May–June).

How to Reach

By Road: Palchha is located ~30 km from Chittorgarh city. Regular bus services and taxis are available from Chittorgarh bus station.
By Rail: Nearest railway station is Chittorgarh Junction. Taxis/auto-rickshaws available for local travel.
By Air: Nearest airport is Udaipur (Dabok) ~100 km away.

Accommodation

Basic guesthouses and small hotels available in Palchha and nearby Chittorgarh. Book in advance during festival seasons. Many visitors prefer homestays for an authentic village experience.

What to Bring

Comfortable walking shoes, light clothing for summer, warm clothes for winters, camera, water bottle and sunscreen. During monsoon carry an umbrella.

Local Tips

Visit temples early morning for a peaceful experience. Interact with locals to learn village traditions. Try local cuisine at small eateries. Respect local customs and dress modestly when visiting temples.

Places to Visit

Mata Ji Temple

Sacred temple dedicated to the Mother Goddess. Best visited during Navratri season. Peaceful spiritual atmosphere and beautiful surroundings.

Mahadev (Shiva) Temple

Ancient temple dedicated to Lord Shiva. Serene location ideal for meditation and spiritual reflection. Special atmosphere during Shravan month.

Hanuman Temple

Devotional temple symbolizing strength and courage. Active worship site with regular prayers. Good starting point to explore the village.

Charbuja Nath Temple

Calm and divine temple dedicated to Lord Charbuja Nath. Known for fulfilling devotees' wishes. Holds regular Bhajan Sandhya on full moon nights.

Village Fields & Nature

Walk through fertile farmlands and experience village life. Beautiful sunrise/sunset views. Great for photography and connecting with nature.

Ancient Stone Inscriptions

Historical Shilalekh (stone inscriptions) near the bus stand and temples. Reflect the ancient heritage and historical identity of Palchha.

Pangadh Lake Retreat

Scenic freshwater lake surrounded by natural beauty. Ideal for picnics, bird watching and quiet reflection. Perfect spot for sunset viewing and photography.

Arawali Hills

Majestic mountain range offering scenic trekking trails and panoramic views. Rich biodiversity and peaceful environment. Best visited in cooler months for comfortable hiking.

Arawali Wildlife & Nature

The Arawali Hills surrounding Palchha are home to diverse wildlife and pristine natural beauty. Monkeys, birds, and various species thrive in this protected ecosystem. Best explored on nature walks and trekking expeditions.
